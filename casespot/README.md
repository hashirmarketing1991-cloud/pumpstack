## Before running Google Ads

- Cash on delivery and 2 to 4 day delivery are promised all over the page. Make sure both are true.
- The old page showed "4.8 out of 5, 2,146 ratings" and named reviews. They were left out on purpose. Only add reviews that are real (paste a Judge.me or Loox widget in the Proof section).
- Images were placed based on how the old page used them. Swap any that look wrong in the theme editor.
