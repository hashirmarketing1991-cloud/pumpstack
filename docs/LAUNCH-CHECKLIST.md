## 2. Promises the site makes (make each one true or change the copy)

The copy uses these claims because they convert. Each one must match how you actually run the business.

- **Free US shipping over $75.** Set a free rate for orders of $75+ in Settings > Shipping. The Full Pump Stack ($94.99) clears it on its own, and so does any 2-pack of pre-workout.
- **30-day money-back guarantee.** Write a refund policy that matches it in Settings > Policies.
- **Third-party tested, every batch.** Your quote board has Eurofins at $900/lot for whey and $300/lot for creatine. Budget for it on every SKU, or soften the claim to "third-party tested" without "every batch".
- **Ships in 1 to 2 business days, arrives in 3 to 5.** Only true with US stock (a 3PL or DDP to Los Angeles). Edit the FAQ if not.
- **"Most popular" / "Bestseller" badges.** Fine as merchandising at launch. Drop "Most popular" if it is not true after 60 days of data.

## 3. FDA / FTC compliance (already applied, keep it this way)

- Every product description uses **structure/function claims only** ("supports", "helps"). There are no disease claims.
- The **DSHEA disclaimer** is on every product and on the homepage features section.
- The pre-workout has **caffeine and age warnings**. Add the exact caffeine mg once the formula is final.
- **No fake reviews or ratings** anywhere. Add a real reviews app (Judge.me or Okendo) and turn on post-purchase review requests. Do not import or invent reviews: the FTC's 2024 fake review rule applies.
- Before launch, **confirm your manufacturer is FDA-registered and cGMP compliant**, and get it in writing. Your US importer of record must file FDA Prior Notice for every shipment from China.

## 5. Getting to a 7 to 8% conversion rate

The store is built for it, but the rate depends mostly on traffic quality. Supplement DTC stores usually convert 2 to 4% overall. Warm and retargeting traffic plus email/SMS can hit 7 to 8%. Cold Meta traffic will not. Measure CVR by traffic source, not blended.

Built in already:
- One clear offer and CTA above the fold, trust bar right under the hero
- Pack-size cards with savings shown in dollars and percent, plus strike-through prices
- One-tap add to cart on every product card
- A bundle offer on the homepage and on every product page
- Sticky add to cart and a cart drawer (Horizon native), and Shop Pay express checkout on the buy box
- Objection handling in order: comparison table, guarantee, FAQ (with FAQPage schema for SEO)
- Mobile-first layout, with no apps slowing the page down

Add next, in this order:
1. **Reviews app** with photo reviews. Social proof is the biggest gap right now.
2. **Subscribe and save** (Shopify Subscriptions app, free). Supplements are a replenishment product, so subscriptions lift both CVR and LTV.
3. **Klaviyo pop-up** (10% off for email + SMS) plus abandoned cart and browse abandonment flows.
4. **Free shipping progress bar** in the cart drawer, to push AOV over $75.
5. **Post-purchase upsell** (for example the gummies at 20% off) with a one-click upsell app.
6. After 1,000 sessions, run A/B tests: hero headline, then default pack selection, then bundle price.
